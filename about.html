WHO WE ARE

Solar System Specialists in Kwekwe

Ringotech is a solar-focused company based in Kwekwe, Zimbabwe, providing solar system installation, repair, inverter services and maintenance.

We help homeowners, businesses and other customers install, maintain and troubleshoot solar power systems.

Our focus is on practical and reliable solar power solutions, from solar panels and batteries to inverters and the electrical systems that connect them.

OUR APPROACH

Reliable Solar Power Starts With the Right System

Every solar installation is different. The required panels, inverter, batteries and electrical connections depend on the customer's power requirements.

We assess the solar system and identify the appropriate installation, repair or maintenance requirements.

When a solar system develops a fault, we troubleshoot the system to identify the source of the problem and determine the required repair.
